      Approach

      Measure. Design. Build. Prove.

      A repeatable engineering loop that replaces guesswork with evidence.

      1. 1

        Measure

        Profile the real workload on real hardware. We establish a baseline, find the bottleneck, and agree on a performance budget.

      2. 2

        Design

        Choose the algorithm, data layout and system topology that fit the budget. Trade-offs are written down, reviewed and revisited.

      3. 3

        Build

        Incremental delivery in small, reviewable changes — tested, sanitized and benchmarked from the first commit.

      4. 4

        Prove

        Benchmark gates in CI, load tests and dashboards demonstrate the gains — and keep them as the codebase evolves.
